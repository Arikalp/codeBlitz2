Core Capabilities

Everything your health journey needs

Built with one goal: ensuring the doctor you see today has the context they need from your entire health history.

Unified Health Timeline

All your encounters, prescriptions, lab results, and discharge summaries in a single chronological view — searchable, indexed, and filterable.

Patient-Controlled Consent

You decide exactly which records to share, with which provider, and for how long. Revoke access anytime with immediate zero-knowledge invalidation.

Multi-Hospital Continuity

When you visit Hospital B, the doctor can review your Hospital A records (with your permission) and add a new encounter to your timeline without data loss.

AI-Assisted Explanations

Plain-language summaries of your reports powered by Groq LLM — always grounded strictly in your verified records with clinical references and warnings.

Document Understanding & OCR

Upload scanned prescriptions or PDFs. Structured clinical data is extracted via OCR and you review every detail before committing it to your longitudinal health record.

ABDM-Inspired Flow (Demo)

Simulated ABHA-style consent and record-retrieval flow for hackathon demonstration — clearly labeled, synthetic, and compliant with national digital health standards.

How HealthSetu Works

A seamless five-step handover loop that keeps patient authority at the very center.

01 Origin

Visit Hospital A

Clinician conducts checkup, orders lab reports (e.g. HbA1c), and prescribes medication.

Apollo Hospitals
02 Patient Vault

You Control Records

Records land in your longitudinal timeline. You view OCR extractions and confirm accuracy.

Patient App / Portal
03 Consent Pin

Grant Consent to B

Hospital B requests consultation access. You review duration (48h) and approve specific scopes.

ABDM Artefact Generated
04 Continuity

Doctor Reviews Context

Hospital B doctor sees your prior HbA1c 7.1% and previous prescriptions before recommending treatment.

Zero Redundant Tests
05 Provenance

New Encounter Added

Hospital B clinician appends new encounter. The timeline grows longitudinally without overwriting.

Interoperability Framework

Aligned with ABDM Protocols & FHIR Specifications

HealthSetu is built to emulate India's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) guidelines. Data is never aggregated in a centralized health honeypot — instead, records travel through encrypted federated exchange channels triggered exclusively by cryptographic patient consent.

  • ✓ FHIR R4 DiagnosticReport & MedicationRequest Resources
  • ✓ Granular consent parameters (purpose, duration, record types)
  • ✓ Groq Llama-3 assisted plain-language patient explanations
  • ✓ Tamper-evident audit logging for all record views
